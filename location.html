Where

Norfolk, Nebraska

The Cowboy Gravel Grinder rolls out of Norfolk, in the rolling hills of northeast Nebraska. Expect quiet gravel roads, wide-open prairie, and the small-town communities that surround the city.

Open in Google Maps ↗

Start & Finish

All routes start and end at Divots Brewery & Norfolk Lodge and Suites — where a well-earned drink and good company await after the ride.

The Cowboy Trail

Norfolk is the eastern trailhead of the Cowboy Trail, a rail-trail stretching west across northern Nebraska. The Short Line Shuffle follows it out to Battle Creek and back.

Getting There

Norfolk sits roughly two hours northwest of Omaha by car. Riders flying in can use Eppley Airfield (OMA) in Omaha.
